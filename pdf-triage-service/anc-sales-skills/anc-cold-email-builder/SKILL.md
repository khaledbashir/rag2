# ANC Cold Email Builder

Write a credible one-to-one ANC email that earns the next conversation. The skill has two doors and never asks the user to choose a mode:

- A draft is pasted -> grade and rebuild it.
- A writing request is given -> build the email from available CRM/research context.

## Context order

Use, in order:

1. Current Person, Company, and Opportunity context.
2. Verified ANC relationship history, previous projects, active service context, and recent activity.
3. Dated public research from `anc-company-researcher`.
4. User-provided facts.

Never claim familiarity, activity, a project, a problem, or a personal detail that the evidence does not prove. Merge fields are not personalization.

## Build

1. Identify the recipient's likely responsibility and the relevant ANC business unit.
2. Choose one defensible lead move: timely signal, known relationship, specific problem, peer proof, concrete offer, or useful insight.
3. Write one honest subject. No fake `Re:`, surveillance language, clickbait, or unsupported name-dropping.
4. Keep the body concise and plain-text in feel:
   - recipient-led opening;
   - one relevant problem or opportunity;
   - one credible ANC outcome/proof point when verified;
   - one low-friction call to action.
5. Use a stage-appropriate CTA. A cold first touch asks for interest or the right owner; an active deal may ask for a specific meeting or decision.
6. Do not add links or attachments unless they are necessary and the user supplied/approved them.

## Grade

Score the actual draft from 0–100 across:

- subject honesty and clarity — 15;
- relevance / recipient-led opening — 25;
- specificity and credibility — 20;
- value and proof — 15;
- clarity and brevity — 10;
- CTA fit — 10;
- deliverability / trust — 5.

Apply caps:

- deceptive or fake-thread subject -> maximum 55;
- sender-led generic pitch with no recipient relevance -> maximum 65;
- missing or high-friction CTA -> maximum 72;
- invented personalization or unsupported proof -> maximum 40 and remove the claim.

Return the score, what works, what costs replies, and a complete rewrite. Keep the explanation plain and specific.

## Output

Always provide:

`Subject:`

Email body

`Why this angle:` one or two sentences tied to evidence.

For grading, add `Score: N/100` and no more than three priority fixes.

## Safety and CRM behavior

- Draft only. Never send, schedule, sequence, or bulk-message without a separate explicit instruction and a connected approved sending workflow.
- Do not expose internal notes or confidential account details in an external email.
- Respect opt-outs and suppression signals. Do not help evade spam controls or misrepresent identity.
- Save the draft as a note or task only after approval; read it back after saving.
