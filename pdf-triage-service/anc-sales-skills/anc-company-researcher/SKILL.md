# ANC Company Researcher

Understand an account well enough to make the next commercial conversation sharper. Use the same evidence standard for one company or a batch; larger batches may be shallower only when the user explicitly asks for a fast scan.

## Source order

1. Current CRM Company, People, Opportunities, activities, notes, service context, and prior wins/losses.
2. First-party sources: company/venue/team sites, official announcements, procurement pages, league sources, and public filings.
3. Reputable trade or local reporting.
4. Search snippets only as discovery leads, never as final evidence.

Attach a URL and date to every public claim that could change. Do not cite a source that does not support the statement.

## Research workflow

1. Resolve the Company and deduplicate possible CRM matches.
2. Read the existing ANC relationship before researching the open web.
3. Establish what the organization does, venue footprint, ownership/operating model, scale indicators, and relevant decision structure.
4. Find `why now` signals: venue project, capital plan, renovation, rights change, technology refresh, service issue, sponsorship motion, event growth, procurement activity, or leadership change.
5. Map the opportunity to one or more ANC business units. Do not force fit.
6. Identify likely buying roles and known CRM People. Distinguish a verified person from a role hypothesis.
7. Assess fit:
   - `Strong` — clear ANC need, credible timing signal, and reachable relationship path;
   - `Fair` — relevant need but timing, access, or budget evidence is incomplete;
   - `Longshot` — weak fit, no defensible signal, or major access/conflict risk.
8. Recommend one conversation angle and one next action.

## Single-company brief

Return:

- `Relationship now` — verified ANC history and open motion.
- `Why now` — strongest dated signals.
- `ANC fit` — business unit, rating, and reason.
- `Buying committee` — known People first, role gaps second.
- `Conversation angle` — a specific problem/outcome, not a generic company introduction.
- `Risks / unknowns` — facts that could change the approach.
- `Next move` — one action.
- `Sources` — direct links.

## Batch research

Return one ranked list with consistent fields for every company. Never refuse a long list solely because of size. State the requested depth, count, and expected completeness before a material long-running or paid operation.

## CRM writes

Research is read-only by default. Save a note, update a field, create a task, or create a new Company only after explicit approval. Preserve citations and observation dates in saved research. Never overwrite a user-confirmed CRM fact with an inference.
